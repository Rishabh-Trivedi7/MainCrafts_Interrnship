About Nivana

Luxury Stays & Experiences Across India

Nivana is a modern hospitality platform focused on peaceful stays, immersive destinations and curated experiences designed for travelers seeking comfort, calm and meaningful journeys.

What Is Nivana?

Nivana helps travelers discover luxury stays, boutique retreats, mountain hideaways, wellness escapes and peaceful destinations across India.

Instead of overwhelming users with endless listings, Nivana focuses on carefully curated experiences that combine comfort, nature and modern luxury in a simple and meaningful way.
